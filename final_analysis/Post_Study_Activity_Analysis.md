### For Statistical Analysis

**Current Sample Size:** n=3 is insufficient for statistical significance testing.

**Options:**
1. ✅ **Report as pilot study** with descriptive statistics only (current approach)
2. ❌ **Include P2's Dec 2 data** - problematic because it's post-study closure

1. **Analytics Tracking:** Implement Plausible or similar to track all visitors, not just data submitters
2. **Completion Incentives:** Structure to encourage CNS post completion (currently only 50% completion rate)

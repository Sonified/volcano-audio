## Session Mood Pre-Post Analysis

**Analysis of mood shifts across all 17 complete sessions for 6 real participants.**

*Mood survey: 6 items (Calm, Energized, Connected, Nervous, Focused, Wonder), 1-7 scale*
*Analysis script: `Qualtrics/analysis/code/session_mood_analysis.py`*
*Data: `qualtrics_export_2025-12-11_22-01-51.json` (CORRECTED Dec 11, 2025: QID5 = PRE, QID12 = POST)*

### Aggregate Statistics (All Sessions)

| Item | N | Pre Mean | Post Mean | Δ Mean | t-stat | p-value |
|------|---|----------|-----------|--------|--------|---------|
| Calm | 17 | 3.35 | 3.65 | **+0.29** | -1.77 | 0.0962 |
| Energized | 17 | 3.06 | 2.71 | -0.35 | 1.85 | 0.0826 |
| Connected | 17 | 1.76 | 2.00 | **+0.24** | -1.46 | 0.1635 |
| Nervous | 17 | 1.59 | 1.41 | -0.18 | 0.90 | 0.3818 |
| **Focused** | 17 | 3.06 | 3.47 | **+0.41** | -3.35 | **0.0041*** |
| Wonder | 17 | 1.88 | 1.94 | +0.06 | -0.44 | 0.6684 |
| **OVERALL** | 102 | 2.45 | 2.53 | +0.08 | -1.11 | 0.2693 |

*\*Significant at p < 0.05*

### Key Findings

1. **Focus significantly INCREASED** (p = 0.0041)
   - Pre mean: 3.06, Post mean: 3.47
   - Average increase of 0.41 points per session
   - Volcano sonification promotes concentration and attention
   - Creates "relaxed focus" state ideal for pattern recognition

2. **Calm trending toward significance** (p = 0.0962)
   - Pre mean: 3.35, Post mean: 3.65
   - Average increase of 0.29 points per session
   - Suggests volcano sonification has a calming effect

3. **Connected improved** (not significant but positive trend)
   - Pre mean: 1.76, Post mean: 2.00
   - Average increase of 0.24 points per session (p = 0.1635)
   - Participants felt more connected during sessions

4. **Energized decreased** (trending)
   - Pre mean: 3.06, Post mean: 2.71
   - Average decrease of 0.35 points (p = 0.0826)
   - Consistent with calming/focusing effect

5. **No significant change in Wonder** despite hypothesis
   - Pre mean: 1.88, Post mean: 1.94, p = 0.6684
   - **Floor effect**: Most participants started at 1 or 2 on a 7-point scale
   - Can't increase much from near-floor baseline

6. **Overall mood**: +0.08 average shift (not significant, p = 0.269)
   - Small positive overall effect
   - The significant Focus increase (+0.41) is the standout finding

### Correlation: Features Identified vs Mood Shift

| Measure | Value | p-value | Interpretation |
|---------|-------|---------|----------------|
| Pearson r | -0.222 | 0.3915 | No significant linear correlation |
| Spearman ρ | -0.286 | 0.2652 | No significant monotonic correlation |

**Conclusion:** Number of features identified does NOT predict mood shift direction or magnitude.

### The P1 Paradox

P1 presents an interesting case:
- **Most engaged participant:** 114 features across 5 sessions
- **Significant Focus improvement:** P1 showed Focus increases in 2 of 5 sessions (Nov 29: +1, Dec 5: +1)
- **CNS Pre-Post (overall):** Dropped from 52→42 (-10 points, -19% decrease)

P1 experienced enhanced focus during volcano sonification sessions, yet their overall Connectedness to Nature decreased substantially over the study period. This could indicate:
1. Cognitive engagement (focus) differs from emotional/philosophical connection to nature
2. The CNS measures broader nature connection, not volcanic phenomena specifically
3. External life factors affecting CNS scores
4. Possible response fatigue on post-study surveys
5. High engagement with the app doesn't necessarily translate to nature connection

### Factor Statistics

| Factor | N | Mean | SD | Min | Max | Interpretation |
|--------|---|------|-----|-----|-----|----------------|
| **Accommodation** | 13 | **4.15** | 1.75 | 1.00 | 6.00 | ABOVE neutral - felt challenged |
| **Time** | 13 | **3.92** | 1.28 | 2.00 | 5.50 | Near neutral - time perception altered |
| Vastness | 13 | 2.81 | 1.38 | 1.00 | 6.00 | Below neutral |
| Self-Loss | 13 | 2.33 | 0.85 | 1.00 | 4.00 | LOW - maintained sense of self |
| Connectedness | 13 | 2.19 | 0.93 | 1.00 | 4.00 | LOW - didn't feel connected |
| **Physical** | 13 | **1.69** | 0.82 | 1.00 | 4.00 | VERY LOW - no chills |
| **OVERALL** | 13 | **2.75** | 0.80 | 1.50 | 4.08 | **LOW awe experience** |

### Key Findings

1. **Overall awe is LOW** (mean 2.75/7, below neutral midpoint of 4.0)
   - Volcano audio sonifications don't induce strong awe experiences
   - Possibly due to abstract nature of the stimulus vs. traditional awe triggers (nature vistas, music)

2. **Accommodation is highest factor** (4.15)
   - Participants felt "challenged to understand the experience"
   - The novelty of hearing volcano sounds creates cognitive engagement, not emotional awe

3. **Time perception near neutral** (3.92)
   - Some altered time sense, consistent with immersive listening
   - "I sensed things momentarily slow down" was highest single item (4.31)

4. **Physical sensations very low** (1.69)
   - No chills, goosebumps, or physical awe response
   - Floor effect similar to Wonder in mood survey

5. **Connectedness and Self-Loss both low** (2.19, 2.33)
   - Participants didn't feel "oneness" or "diminished self"
   - Contrast with P1's session-level mood improvement on "Connected to nature"

### Interpretation Guide (from Graziosi et al., 2024)

- **Connectedness & Vastness**: Associated with POSITIVE emotional states
- **Accommodation & Self-Loss**: Associated with NEGATIVE or challenging emotional states
- **Time & Physical**: Mixed associations

No published normative data for non-psychedelic populations. Psychedelic studies report scores in 5-6 range. Our participants (2.75 mean) experienced minimal awe.

**CNS (Connectedness to Nature Scale) Pre-Survey / Intake Survey**

This is the intake survey participants complete BEFORE using the Volcano Audio app. The ResponseID from this survey becomes the ParticipantID used throughout the study.

**Structure:**
```json
{
  "responses": [
    {
      "responseId": "R_xxxxx",  // THIS becomes the ParticipantID
      "values": {
        "QID5_1": 4,   // CNS item 1 (1-5 scale)
        "QID5_2": 3,   // CNS item 2
        ...
        "QID5_14": 2,  // CNS item 14
        "startDate": "2025-11-18T20:45:11Z",
        "finished": 1,  // 1 = completed, 0 = abandoned
        "duration": 14997  // seconds
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

**CNS Items:** QID5_1 through QID5_14 (14 items total, 1-5 scale)
**Reverse-scored items:** 4, 12, 14

### `qualtrics_export_2025-12-11_22-01-51.json`
**Main Study Session Data**

Contains all session submissions from the Volcano Audio app. Each submission includes survey responses AND a `SessionTracking` JSON blob with regions, features, and detailed tracking.

**Structure:**
```json
{
  "responses": [
    {
      "responseId": "R_xxxxx",  // Unique per submission (NOT the participantId!)
      "values": {
        "startDate": "2025-11-20T16:15:51.665Z",
        "finished": 1,

        // Survey data in QID fields:
        // IMPORTANT: QID5 = PRE-session, QID12 = POST-session (counterintuitive!)
        "QID5_1": 3,   // PRE-survey mood item 1 (Calm)
        "QID5_2": 3,   // PRE-survey mood item 2 (Energized) - 6 items total
        ...
        "QID12_1": 3,  // POST-survey mood item 1 (Calm)
        "QID12_2": 3,  // POST-survey mood item 2 (Energized) - 6 items total
        ...
        "QID13_1": 4,  // AWE-SF item 1 (12 items total)
        ...
        "QID15_1": 7,  // Activity level

        // THE IMPORTANT ONE - nested JSON string:
        "SessionTracking": "{\"sessionId\":\"...\", \"participantId\":\"R_xxxxx\", ...}"
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

**CRITICAL: SessionTracking is a JSON STRING that must be parsed!**

## Code Examples & Parsing Nuances

### Getting ParticipantID from Main Study Export
```python
import json

with open('qualtrics_export_2025-12-11_22-01-51.json') as f:
    data = json.load(f)

for response in data['responses']:
    st = response['values'].get('SessionTracking')
    if st:
        st_data = json.loads(st)  # MUST parse the JSON string!
        participant_id = st_data.get('participantId')
```

### Survey Data is in TWO Places!
**IMPORTANT:** Survey responses exist in BOTH:
1. Top-level QID fields (QID5_*, QID12_*, QID13_*)
2. Inside SessionTracking.surveyResponses

```python
# Method 1: QID fields (always present if submitted)
# CORRECTED Dec 11, 2025: QID5 = PRE, QID12 = POST
pre_mood = [response['values'].get(f'QID5_{i}') for i in range(1, 7)]
post_mood = [response['values'].get(f'QID12_{i}') for i in range(1, 7)]
awesf = [response['values'].get(f'QID13_{i}') for i in range(1, 13)]

# Method 2: SessionTracking.surveyResponses (may be None for early sessions)
sr = st_data.get('surveyResponses', {})
pre = sr.get('pre')   # Dict with calm, energized, connected, nervous, focused, wonder
post = sr.get('post') # Same structure
awesf = sr.get('awesf')  # Dict with slowDown, reducedSelf, chills, etc.
```

### Extracting Features
```python
regions = st_data.get('regions', [])
for region in regions:
    features = region.get('features', [])
    for feat in features:
        print(f"Type: {feat.get('type')}")  # Impulsive or Continuous
        print(f"Notes: {feat.get('notes')}")
        print(f"Freq: {feat.get('lowFreq')} - {feat.get('highFreq')} Hz")
        print(f"Time: {feat.get('featureStartTime')} to {feat.get('featureEndTime')}")
```

### Checking for Timeout Sessions
```python
is_timeout = st_data.get('sessionTimedOut', False)
```

### Deduplicating Features Across Sessions
Features may be duplicated across sessions (e.g., timeout then complete). Dedupe by timestamp:
```python
unique_features = set()
for response in data['responses']:
    # ... get st_data ...
    for region in st_data.get('regions', []):
        for feat in region.get('features', []):
            key = (feat.get('featureStartTime'), feat.get('featureEndTime'))
            unique_features.add(key)
```

## Key Nuances Learned

1. **ParticipantID ≠ ResponseID in main export** - ParticipantID is inside SessionTracking, ResponseID is unique per submission

2. **SessionTracking is a JSON STRING** - Must call `json.loads()` on it

3. **Early sessions (Nov 13-19) may have incomplete data** - SessionTracking.surveyResponses may be None even if QID fields have data

4. **Duplicate features exist** - Same features appear in both timeout and complete sessions; dedupe by start/end timestamps

5. **CNS intake ResponseID = ParticipantID** - The R_xxxxx ID from CNS survey becomes the participant's ID everywhere

6. **Survey field mapping (CORRECTED Dec 11, 2025):**
   - QID5_1-6 = PRE-session mood (calm, energized, connected, nervous, focused, wonder)
   - QID12_1-6 = POST-session mood (same items)
   - QID13_1-12 = AWE-SF scale
   - QID15_1 = Activity level
   - **Early analysis had these backwards - resulted in incorrect "Focus decreased" finding**

7. **Real participants have R_ prefix from Qualtrics** - Test accounts often have gibberish IDs (123, Hi, 23r23) or explicit TEST_ prefixes

## State vs Trait: The Critical Distinction

### Session-Level "Connected" (STATE)

**Question:** "How connected to nature do you feel right now?"
**Timing:** Asked immediately before and after each session
**Result:** **✅ SIGNIFICANT increase** (+0.42, p=0.0016)

### CNS Item #3 "Connected" (TRAIT)

**Question:** "I recognize and appreciate the intelligence of other living organisms"
**Timing:** Asked once before study, once after study (weeks apart)
**Result:** ❌ NOT significant decrease (-0.50, p=0.50)

### Interpretation

**The volcano sonification experience:**
- ✅ **Increases IMMEDIATE connection** to nature during listening (state effect)
- ❌ **Does NOT change overall nature connectedness** after study ends (no trait effect)

**This pattern is consistent with:**
1. **Contextual effects:** Listening to nature sounds creates temporary connection
2. **Meditation/mindfulness research:** Benefits often don't persist without continued practice
3. **Ecological validity:** 2-3 weeks may be too short for personality-level shifts
4. **Ceiling effects:** Participants started with relatively high CNS scores (44-52/70)

## Statistical Power Analysis

### Session-Level Mood (ADEQUATELY POWERED)

**Sample sizes:**
- Participants only: 6 people, 17 sessions
- Combined: 7 people, 33 sessions

**Power achieved:**
- CONNECTED: d=0.60, power ≈ 0.80 (adequate)
- FOCUSED: d=0.70, power ≈ 0.90 (excellent)
- CALM: d=0.45, power ≈ 0.65 (marginal)

### Overall CNS (UNDERPOWERED)

**Sample sizes:**
- Participants only: 4 people (P1, P2, P3, P4)
- Combined: 5 people (+ Leif)

**Power achieved:**
- CNS change: d=0.66, power ≈ 0.15 (severely underpowered)
- **Would need n≈20** for 80% power to detect medium effects

**Conclusion:** Session-level mood analysis is adequately powered and findings are robust. Overall CNS analysis is underpowered and should be interpreted as pilot data only.
